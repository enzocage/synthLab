# SynthLab – Nano Banana 2 prompts for all synthesizers

This document contains one production-ready image-generation prompt for every
engine registered in SynthLab. The prompts are written in English because image
models generally interpret technical product-photography language more
consistently in English.

## Shared generation settings

Use these settings for every image so the Synth Pics gallery looks like one
coherent collection:

- Landscape image, 16:9.
- One synthesizer only, fully visible.
- Front three-quarter view from slightly above.
- Product centered horizontally with breathing room around it.
- Dark neutral studio background.
- Soft key light from the upper left and a restrained rim light.
- Realistic materials, controls, shadows, reflections, and manufacturing detail.
- No people, hands, cables crossing the foreground, stands, furniture, packaging,
  floating parts, or decorative text.
- No extra readable brand names, logos, watermarks, signatures, captions, or UI mockup
  frames.
- Controls may use tiny abstract markings, but avoid generated pseudo-words.
- Keep the front panel readable at thumbnail size.
- Photorealistic product photography, not illustration or concept art.
- Native output should be at least 2048 × 1152 pixels.

Recommended shared suffix:

```text
Photorealistic premium product photography, landscape 16:9, one complete
instrument centered in frame, front three-quarter view from slightly above,
dark charcoal seamless studio background, soft large key light from upper left,
subtle cool rim light, realistic metal, plastic, wood and glass, crisp controls,
clean silhouette, restrained reflections, deep but readable shadows, high detail,
sharp focus across the full front panel, no people, no hands, no stand, no loose
cables, no packaging, no scenery, no text overlay, no readable logos, no
watermark, no duplicate instrument, no cropped keyboard, no fisheye distortion.
Author credit: SynthLab, by enzo cage.
```

## Export checklist

For each generated image:

1. Confirm that the entire instrument is visible and not cropped.
2. Reject outputs with misspelled panel text or invented brand logos.
3. Reject duplicate keyboards, malformed keys, impossible knobs, extra hands,
   floating cables or asymmetric chassis errors.
4. Keep the subject scale and camera angle consistent across all 23 images.
5. Export as sRGB PNG or high-quality JPEG.
6. Use the filename suggested under each prompt.
7. Preserve the original generation at full resolution before creating the
   smaller web-delivery version.
8. For SynthLab, target a web version around 1600 × 900 and preferably below
   500 KB per image.
