# Open-Source FM Synthesizer Reference Research

This document logs the 5 top open-source Web Audio FM synthesizer repository references integrated into `synthlab`.

## 1. DX7 6-Operator FM (`fm-dx7`)
- **Reference:** [`ms3000/DX7-WebAudio`](https://github.com/ms3000/DX7-WebAudio) / `Tone.js` DX7 FM
- **License:** MIT
- **Architecture:** 6-Operator frequency modulation matrix supporting classic Yamaha DX7 algorithm routing, envelope generators & ratio tuning.

## 2. 4-Operator Retro FM (`fm-4op`)
- **Reference:** [`g200kg/webaudio-tinysynth`](https://github.com/g200kg/webaudio-tinysynth) & Sega Genesis YM2612 hardware specs
- **License:** MIT
- **Architecture:** 4-Operator FM with feedback path on Op 1. Ideal for crunchy 80s arcade, Lately Bass, and punchy FM keys.

## 3. Morphing FM (`fm-morph`)
- **Reference:** [`Ameobea/web-synth`](https://github.com/Ameobea/web-synth)
- **License:** MIT
- **Architecture:** Dynamic FM with continuous phase offset & operator morphing for evolving ambient textures.

## 4. High-Feedback Chaos FM (`fm-feedback`)
- **Reference:** [`ThomasFoydel/fmsynth`](https://github.com/ThomasFoydel/fmsynth)
- **License:** MIT
- **Architecture:** Dual self-modulating FM loops with non-linear saturation for metallic noise, industrial transients & heavy bass.

## 5. Linear Harmonic Precision FM (`fm-linear`)
- **Reference:** [`ToneJS/Tone.js`](https://github.com/Tonejs/Tone.js) (`Tone.FMSynth`)
- **License:** MIT
- **Architecture:** High-precision linear harmonic ratio modulation for crystalline bells, glass chimes & pristine pads.
